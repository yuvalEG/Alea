# Privacy policy

**Alea Scale Shifter** and **Alea Chord Randomizer** (together, "the apps").

Last updated: 14 August 2026.

## The short version

The apps collect nothing about you. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no
advertising, no crash reporting, no unique identifiers. Nothing you do in them
is sent anywhere, and nothing about you is stored outside your own computer.

## What the apps store, and where

Your settings and your saved patches live on your own device, in the ordinary
places an app keeps its preferences, and in any files you choose to save
yourself. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them.

## The one time the apps use the network

The desktop versions can contact the internet in exactly one situation: when
**you** choose "Check for Updates" from the menu. That asks GitHub which
version is the newest, so the app can tell you whether yours is current.

- It happens only when you ask for it. Nothing is checked in the background.
- The request contains no personal information and nothing about how you use
  the app. It is a plain request for a public list of released versions.
- GitHub, like any web server, will see the request and the network address it
  came from. That is GitHub's ordinary server logging, described in their own
  privacy statement.

Beyond that one user-initiated check, the apps never connect to anything. They
have no background activity, no telemetry and no "phone home" of any kind.

## Children

The apps are suitable for all ages and collect nothing from anyone, including
children.

## Third-party code

The apps are built with the JUCE framework, and include the Upright Piano KW
samples from the FreePats project (CC0, public domain). Neither collects data
through the apps.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a
new date above.
